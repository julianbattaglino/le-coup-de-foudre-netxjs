# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People planning considered, private journeys through France, Argentina, and Uruguay who are looking for inspiration before contacting Le Coup de Foudre.

## Product Purpose

Le Coup de Foudre presents curated private travel experiences and helps visitors discover destinations before making a concierge enquiry.

## Positioning

The maison frames a journey as a sequence of intimate, culturally grounded places rather than a catalogue of generic travel products.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing Next.js site contains destination pages for France, Argentina, and Uruguay. The interactive journey must link to those pages, work on touch devices, retain a reduced-motion experience, and avoid a heavy persistent 3D dependency.

## Brand Commitments

Editorial Parisian maison; private journeys; restrained, elegant, and culturally attentive voice.

## Evidence on Hand

17 destination records in `data/destinations.json`, each with an existing destination route and destination imagery.

## Product Principles

- Let exploration feel like a journey, not filtering a database.
- Make every destination a direct route into a richer story.
- Keep the experience calm, legible, and useful on every device.
