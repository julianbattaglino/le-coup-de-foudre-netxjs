# Le Coup de Foudre Paris — Home

## Direction

An editorial Parisian maison for private journeys through French savoir-faire. The brand name is the first visual event: a monumental two-line wordmark in the nocturne field, followed by large photographic compositions and a discreet concierge enquiry.

## Visual system

- Ground: warm ivory `#F9F8F5`; secondary field: warm ivory `#F4F1EA`.
- Ink: carbon `#1C1D1C`; nocturne field: `#162238`; brand navy: `#1B2A4A`.
- Accent: muted brass `#B89758`, used for small details and focus states.
- Display: Playfair Display. Narrative: EB Garamond. Interface: Montserrat.
- Square frames, fine dividers, wide tracking for interface labels, and large type with compact leading.
- Motion stays limited to the wordmark entrance, button feedback, and image color response. Reduced motion keeps the page fully visible and still.

## Page scope

One responsive home route with anchor navigation, a photographic experience gallery, three journey formats, a maison story, and a concierge request form that opens a prefilled email. No other routes or persistent data are part of this first pass.

## Content notes

The “1,500+ experiences” statement and concierge address are carried over from the supplied Beta mockup and should be confirmed before publication. The photographs are illustrative destination imagery from Unsplash, not photographs of the agency's own itineraries.
